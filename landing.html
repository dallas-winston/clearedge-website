How It Works

Our 6-Step Installation Process

01

Free Consultation

We assess your vehicle, discuss your coverage goals, and recommend the right package for your budget and driving habits.

02

Paint Inspection

We inspect and document your paint's current condition before we begin so there are no disputes about pre-existing damage.

03

Decontamination Wash

Full decontamination wash and clay bar treatment removes all surface contaminants that could affect film adhesion.

04

Custom Film Cutting

Film is computer-cut to your exact vehicle's template for a precise, edge-to-edge fit. No hand-cutting guesswork.

05

Professional Installation

Our certified technicians install the film in our climate-controlled, dust-free facility for a flawless, bubble-free result.

06

Final Walkthrough
